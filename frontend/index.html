Everything You Need

Built for Inspiration

Every feature you need to find, enjoy, and share quotes — in one beautiful app.

9 Live Sources

Quotes are fetched in real-time from 9 independent APIs — ZenQuotes, Quotable, FavQs, and more — shuffled on every request so you always get a fresh, unique line.

Smart Filters

Narrow down by mood (happy, inspiring, peaceful…) and topic (love, success, wisdom…), or pick a specific author. Get exactly the quote you're in the mood for.

Powerful Search

Search by keyword, paste a full quote to find its author, or browse by tag. Results ranked by relevance with instant in-page highlighting.

Text to Speech

Tap Speak to hear any quote read aloud using your device's natural speech engine. Multiple voices supported — great for accessibility, focus, and on-the-go inspiration.

Personal Collection

Save quotes you love with a single tap. Sign in to sync your personal collection across devices and revisit your favorites any time.

One-Click Sharing

Share directly to Twitter and WhatsApp with a single click — quotes are pre-formatted with the author name and ready to post, no editing needed.
