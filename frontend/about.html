Our Mission

QuoteGen was built with a single goal — make the world's wisdom instantly accessible to everyone. We pull quotes from 9 independent sources across the internet in real-time, combine them with a hand-curated database, and present them through a clean, fast interface. No ads, no trackers, no paywalls. Great words should be free.

Everything You Need

What QuoteGen Offers

Every feature was designed around one question: what makes a quote truly memorable?

9 Live Sources

Quotes are fetched in real-time from 9 independent APIs — ZenQuotes, Quotable, DummyJSON, QuoteGarden, Forismatic, Type.fit, Stoic Quotes, FavQs, and Kanye.rest — shuffled and tried in order so you always get a fresh line.

Smart Filters

Narrow down by mood (happy, inspiring, peaceful…) or topic (love, success, wisdom…), or pick a specific author. Filters work independently so you get exactly the quote you're in the mood for.

Text-to-Speech

Tap Speak to hear any quote read aloud using your device's natural speech engine. Supports multiple voices and languages — great for accessibility, focus, and on-the-go inspiration.

Personal Favorites

Save any quote with one tap. Sign in to sync your personal collection across devices via our secure API, or browse favorites any time from the dedicated Favorites page.

Powerful Search

Search by keyword, paste a full quote to find its author, or browse by mood and topic tag. Results are ranked by relevance — exact matches appear first, followed by partial matches.

One-Click Sharing

Share directly to Twitter and WhatsApp in a single click — quotes are pre-formatted with the author's name and ready to post. No copying, no editing required.

Simple & Fast

How It Works

From zero to inspired in under ten seconds.

1

Choose Your Filter

Select a mood, topic, or author from the filter bar — or leave everything open for a surprise. QuoteGen searches thousands of quotes to find your perfect match.

2

Read or Listen

Read the quote and tap Speak to hear it aloud. Use Previous and Next to browse your session history without losing any quote you liked.

3

Save & Share

Tap the heart to save it to your collection, or share instantly to Twitter and WhatsApp. Sign in to keep your favorites synced across every device you use.
